# DALFAST Studio for Claude

Design flyers, social posts, stories, cards, invitations, posters, menus and web banners from DALFAST Studio templates, right from the chat. Then open the design in DALFAST Studio to change colours and pictures, add your logo, print or share.

## What it adds

- **The DALFAST Studio connector** (`https://dalfast.studio/connect/app`): find templates, make a design with your own words, list and open your designs, download them as PDF or PNG, and open them in DALFAST Studio.
- **The "Design with DALFAST" skill:** how Claude picks templates, writes short headlines and details, chooses the right size, and never invents prices, dates or contact details.

## Getting started

1. Add the plugin, then connect your DALFAST Studio account when Claude asks (free to start at https://dalfast.studio).
2. Ask, for example:
   - "Find me a flyer template for a bakery opening this Saturday"
   - "Make a birthday invitation for Ana's 30th, 6 December at 7 pm"
   - "Show my latest DALFAST designs and give me a PDF of the newest"

## Privacy and support

The connector only reads and makes designs in your own account. It never deletes anything or pays for anything. You can end the connection at any time at https://dalfast.studio/connect/apps.
