# DALFAST Studio for Claude

**Designs, videos and marketing for everyone.** Create professional designs and marketing content directly from Claude: tell DALFAST Studio what you want to create, from a social post, flyer, poster or business card to a presentation, menu, invoice or ad. DALFAST Studio finds a fitting template, adds your own words and creates it in your account, ready to open and finish.

Built for creators, businesses, marketers, teams, professionals, entrepreneurs and anyone who wants to create great content faster.

## What it adds

- **The DALFAST Studio connector** (`https://dalfast.studio/connect/app`): find templates, make a design with your own words, list and open your designs, download them as PDF or PNG, and open them in DALFAST Studio.
- **Six skills** that show Claude how to do the common jobs well:
  - **Design with DALFAST:** any design from a template, the right size, short headlines that work.
  - **Flyer or poster:** events, sales, openings, launches and classes, with the details people need.
  - **A week of posts:** a plan of 5 to 7 posts with captions, made as one matching set.
  - **Brand kit:** colours, fonts and a tagline, with a matching business card, letterhead and intro post.
  - **Video from a sentence:** a scene plan, script and cover, then the video is made in DALFAST Studio.
  - **Menu or price list:** every item and price exactly as you give them; long lists carry on to more pages.

Every skill uses only the facts you give: no made-up prices, dates, addresses, reviews or names.

## Also in DALFAST Studio (when you open your creation there)

- **Video editing:** a full editor with tracks, trimming and transitions, colour looks, subtitles, vertical videos for TikTok and Reels, long videos into Shorts, photo slideshows, and screen and camera recording.
- **Images:** photo editing, filters and background removal.
- **Sound:** your own voice recordings, music and sound effects, and volume and mixing for every clip.
- **Quick designs:** social posts and stories, flyers, posters, menus, cards, invitations, thumbnails, web banners, business cards and presentations, from ready-made templates.
- **Your brand:** your logo, colours and fonts in one place, on every design and video.
- **Marketing Studio:** posts and adverts for your products, in your brand.
- **Every size:** one design or video resized for every platform.
- **Share:** download as PDF or PNG, print, or post to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and more.

## Getting started

1. Add the plugin, then connect your DALFAST Studio account when Claude asks (free to start at https://dalfast.studio).
2. Ask, for example:
   - "Make a grand opening flyer for my bakery"
   - "Plan a week of Instagram posts for my new course"
   - "Make a menu for my café: Espresso 2.20, Flat white 3.40, Almond croissant 3.20"
   - "Show my latest DALFAST designs and give me a PDF of the newest"

## Privacy and support
