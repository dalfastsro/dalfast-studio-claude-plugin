# DALFAST Studio for Claude

**Small business design & video.** Marketing designs for small businesses, made from the chat. Built for shops, cafés and restaurants, salons, coaches, trades, local services and creators who need professional flyers, posts and ads fast, in their own brand, without hiring a designer.

## What it adds

- **The DALFAST Studio connector** (`https://dalfast.studio/connect/app`): find templates, make a design with your own words, list and open your designs, download them as PDF or PNG, and open them in DALFAST Studio.
- **The "Design with DALFAST" skill:** how Claude picks templates, writes short headlines and details, chooses the right size, and never invents prices, dates or contact details.

## Also in DALFAST Studio (when you open your design there)

- **Video editing:** a full editor with tracks, trimming and transitions, colour looks, subtitles, vertical videos for TikTok and Reels, long videos into Shorts, photo slideshows, and screen and camera recording.
- **Images:** photo editing, filters and background removal.
- **Sound:** your own voice recordings, music and sound effects, and volume and mixing for every clip.
- **Quick designs:** social posts and stories, flyers, posters, menus, cards, invitations, thumbnails, web banners, business cards and presentations, from ready-made templates.
- **Your brand:** your logo, colours and fonts in one place, on every design and video.
- **Marketing Studio:** posts, adverts and campaigns for your products, in your brand.
- **Every size:** one design or video resized for every platform.
- **Share:** download as PDF or PNG, print, or post to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and more.

## Getting started

1. Add the plugin, then connect your DALFAST Studio account when Claude asks (free to start at https://dalfast.studio).
2. Ask, for example:
   - "Make a flyer for my bakery's grand opening on Saturday"
   - "Make an Instagram post for my salon's Black Friday sale"
   - "Show my latest DALFAST designs and give me a PDF of the newest"

## Privacy and support

The connector only reads and makes designs in your own account. It never deletes anything or pays for anything. You can end the connection at any time at https://dalfast.studio/connect/apps.
