# A week of posts with DALFAST Studio

## Flow

1. Ask once for: **what the posts are about** (a launch, an event, a sale, everyday content), **who they are for**, the **facts they can share** (dates, offer, product names, links), and **where** they post (Instagram feed, Stories, LinkedIn, Facebook).
2. Propose a short plan **in the chat first**: 5 to 7 posts, one line each, each with a different job, for example: announce · show the product or service · a tip or how-to · behind the scenes · a question to the audience · a reminder · last call. Only facts they gave. Let them change it.
3. When they agree, for each post: `find_templates` (format **Instagram post**, or **Story** for stories), then `design_from_template` with a short `title`, a `subtitle` or `body` line, and an `action` when the post asks for one. Use the same template family for the whole week so it looks like one set.
4. Under each design, write the **caption** for the post (2–4 short lines, the call to action, 3–6 relevant hashtags). Captions are text in the chat; they make no claims the person did not give.
5. Give every "Open in DALFAST Studio" link, and a simple schedule (Day · post · time to post if they asked).
6. Tell them that in DALFAST Studio they can add their logo and brand colours to the whole set, resize every post for other platforms in one go, and post or download them.

## Keep it honest

- No invented numbers, results, testimonials or "best in town" claims.
- No other brands' names or logos unless the person owns them.

## Always

- Use only the person's own facts. Never make up prices, dates, times, addresses, phone numbers, names, reviews, awards or discounts. Leave a field out rather than guess, and say what is missing.
- Keep their spelling of names and places. Ask before translating.
- One short question at most when something essential is unclear; otherwise make it and offer changes.
- Every design is saved in the person's own DALFAST Studio account. Give the "Open in DALFAST Studio" link with every design.
- Nothing is ever deleted through the connector.
