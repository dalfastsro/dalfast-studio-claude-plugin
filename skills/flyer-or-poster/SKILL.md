# Flyers and posters with DALFAST Studio

## Flow

1. Get the essentials from the person (ask once, together, only for what is missing): **what is happening**, **when**, **where**, and **what people should do** (come, book, call, visit, buy). A name for the business, group or event if they have one.
2. Pick the size from where it will hang: hand-out flyer = **Flyer** (A5), wall or noticeboard = **Poster** (A4), shop window or event entrance = **Poster** (A3 in the Studio).
3. `find_templates` with the occasion in plain words plus the format, e.g. `{"query": "yoga class poster", "format": "Poster"}`. Offer two or three by name.
4. `design_from_template` with: `brand` (name), `title` (2–6 word headline), `subtitle` (one line on the offer or theme), `details` (date · time · place on one line), `action` (call to action). Leave out what they did not give.
5. Give the "Open in DALFAST Studio" link. Offer the same words as an Instagram post or Story too (one more `design_from_template` with a matching template in that format).
6. Print: `download_design` with `format: pdf`. Social: `format: png`.

## Headlines that work

- Say the news, not the noise: "Grand opening", "Summer sale · up to 30% off" (only if they gave the 30%), "Free first class" (only if it is free).
- A date people can read at a glance: "Sat 28 Nov · 10:00–16:00".

## Always

- Use only the person's own facts. Never make up prices, dates, times, addresses, phone numbers, names, reviews, awards or discounts. Leave a field out rather than guess, and say what is missing.
- Keep their spelling of names and places. Ask before translating.
- One short question at most when something essential is unclear; otherwise make it and offer changes.
- Every design is saved in the person's own DALFAST Studio account. Give the "Open in DALFAST Studio" link with every design.
- Nothing is ever deleted through the connector.
