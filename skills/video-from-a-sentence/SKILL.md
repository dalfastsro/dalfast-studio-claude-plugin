# A video from a sentence with DALFAST Studio

The video itself is made and edited in DALFAST Studio (it has the video editor, subtitles, voice-overs, music and vertical sizes). Through the connector you plan it, write it and make its cover, then open DALFAST Studio to make it.

## Flow

1. Ask once for: **what the video is about**, **who it is for**, **how long** (15, 30 or 60 seconds) and **where** it goes (TikTok, Reels and Shorts are tall 9:16; YouTube is wide 16:9).
2. Write a **scene plan** in the chat: 4–6 scenes, each with what is on screen, the words on screen (short) and what the voice-over says (one sentence). Only facts they gave. Let them change it.
3. Make the **cover or thumbnail**: `find_templates` with format **YouTube thumbnail** (wide) or **Story** (tall), then `design_from_template` with the video's `title` and a short `subtitle`.
4. Give the cover's "Open in DALFAST Studio" link and the `open_in_studio` link, and say exactly what to do there: **Video → From a sentence** (paste the first line of the plan), or **New video** and add their clips or photos; then **Add subtitles**, a voice-over and music, and export in the size they need.
5. Offer a caption and hashtags for posting.

## Keep it honest

- Do not say the video is already made: it is made in DALFAST Studio.
- No invented facts, prices or results in the script.

## Always

- Use only the person's own facts. Never make up prices, dates, times, addresses, phone numbers, names, reviews, awards or discounts. Leave a field out rather than guess, and say what is missing.
- Keep their spelling of names and places. Ask before translating.
- One short question at most when something essential is unclear; otherwise make it and offer changes.
- Every design is saved in the person's own DALFAST Studio account. Give the "Open in DALFAST Studio" link with every design.
- Nothing is ever deleted through the connector.
