# Designing with DALFAST Studio

DALFAST Studio is an online design studio. Through the DALFAST Studio connector you can find templates, make a design from one with the person's own words, list and open their designs, download them, and give a link that opens a design in DALFAST Studio to finish it.

## The usual flow

1. **Understand what it is for.** One short question if it is unclear: what is it for (a sale, an opening, a birthday), and where will it be used (printed flyer, Instagram post, story, web or email banner, poster).
2. **Find templates** with `find_templates`, using what the design is for ("bakery opening flyer", "birthday invitation", "black friday sale"). Add `format` when the person named one (Flyer, Instagram post, Story, Poster, Web banner, Invitation, Menu, Business card).
3. **Show two or three** of the best matches by name and let the person choose. If none fits, search again with different words before giving up.
4. **Make the design** with `design_from_template`, filling `texts` only with what the person gave: business or event name, headline, date and time, place, offer, call to action. Never make up prices, dates, addresses, phone numbers, reviews or names. Leave a field out rather than guess.
5. **Give the link** that opens it in DALFAST Studio, where the person can change colours and pictures, add their logo, print or share.
6. **Download** with `download_design` when they want a PDF (print) or PNG (social). If the answer is a link that opens DALFAST Studio, pass it on as it is: the Studio makes that file.

## Writing good words for a design

- **Headline:** 2 to 6 words, the one thing people must notice ("Grand opening", "Up to 30% off", "Ana turns 30").
- **Details:** date, time and place on one line ("Saturday 28 November · 10 am – 4 pm · 12 Mill Street").
- **Call to action:** a short verb phrase ("Book your table", "Shop the sale", "RSVP by 1 December").
- Keep the person's own spelling of names and places. Ask before translating.

## Sizes, in plain words

- Printed flyer: A5. Poster: A4 or A3 (A3 for a shop window).
- Instagram post: square. Story: tall (9:16). Web or email banner: wide (1500 × 500).
- One design can be made again in another shape: make it from the same template in the other format.

## Good to know

- The person signs in to their own DALFAST Studio account when they connect. Everything is saved there.
- Nothing is ever deleted through the connector; designs made here stay in the person's account.
- Picture-making, brand kits, video and printing are in DALFAST Studio itself: give the "Open in DALFAST Studio" link when the person wants those.
