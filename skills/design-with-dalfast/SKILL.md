# Designing with DALFAST Studio

DALFAST Studio is an online creative studio for designs, video, pictures, sound and marketing. Through the connector you can find templates, make a design from one with the person's own words, list and open their designs, download them, and give a link that opens a design in DALFAST Studio to finish it.

## The usual flow

1. **Understand what it is for** and where it will be used (printed, Instagram, story, web banner, email, poster).
2. **Find templates** with `find_templates` using what it is for ("bakery opening flyer", "birthday invitation", "product launch post"). Add `format` when one was named (Flyer, Poster, Brochure, Menu, Instagram post, Story, Card, Christmas card, Invitation, Presentation, Document, Letterhead, Invoice, CV, Certificate, Business card, Gift voucher, Web banner, YouTube thumbnail, Facebook cover, Desktop wallpaper, Phone wallpaper, Art print).
3. **Show two or three** of the best matches by name and let the person choose. If none fits, search again with other words.
4. **Make it** with `design_from_template`, filling `texts` only with what the person gave.
5. **Give the link** to finish it in DALFAST Studio: colours, pictures, logo, brand, print or share.
6. **Download** with `download_design` for a PDF (print) or PNG (social). If the answer is a link that opens DALFAST Studio, pass it on as it is.

## Words that work on a design

- Headline: 2 to 6 words, the one thing people must notice.
- Details: date, time and place on one line.
- Call to action: a short verb phrase ("Book your table", "Shop the sale", "RSVP by 1 December").

## Always

- Use only the person's own facts. Never make up prices, dates, times, addresses, phone numbers, names, reviews, awards or discounts. Leave a field out rather than guess, and say what is missing.
- Keep their spelling of names and places. Ask before translating.
- One short question at most when something essential is unclear; otherwise make it and offer changes.
- Every design is saved in the person's own DALFAST Studio account. Give the "Open in DALFAST Studio" link with every design.
- Nothing is ever deleted through the connector.
