# A brand kit with DALFAST Studio

## Flow

1. Ask for: the **name**, a **one-line description** of what they do, the **feel** they want (e.g. calm, bold, playful, premium, natural), and any **colours, fonts or logo** they already have.
2. In the chat, suggest a simple brand: 2–3 colours (with hex codes), one heading font and one text font, a short tagline in their words. Explain each choice in one line. Let them change it.
3. Make the starter set, each with `find_templates` + `design_from_template` using the same name and tagline:
   - **Business card** (`brand` = name, `title` = their name or role if given, `details` = only the contact details they gave),
   - **Letterhead**,
   - **Instagram post** introducing the brand (`title` = name, `subtitle` = tagline),
   - optionally a **Web banner** or **Facebook cover**.
4. Give each "Open in DALFAST Studio" link, and the link from `open_in_studio` (no project) so they can open **My Brand** in DALFAST Studio and save the colours, fonts and logo there. Once saved, DALFAST Studio puts their brand on their business designs and videos.
5. A logo picture is made or uploaded in DALFAST Studio; the connector does not create pictures.

## Keep it honest

- Only the contact details, names and claims they gave. Check that the name is theirs to use; do not copy another company's logo or name.

## Always

- Use only the person's own facts. Never make up prices, dates, times, addresses, phone numbers, names, reviews, awards or discounts. Leave a field out rather than guess, and say what is missing.
- Keep their spelling of names and places. Ask before translating.
- One short question at most when something essential is unclear; otherwise make it and offer changes.
- Every design is saved in the person's own DALFAST Studio account. Give the "Open in DALFAST Studio" link with every design.
- Nothing is ever deleted through the connector.
